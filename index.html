❓ How to Play

  1. A word is hidden behind the blanks. The banner tells you what it's about.
  2. Tap a letter. If it's in the word it fills in — every copy of it at once.
  3. If it isn't, the enemy catapult fires and your wall loses a block.
  4. Guess the whole word and the masons rebuild part of the wall, then the next word arrives.
  5. The wall has to last the whole siege, not just one word. Lose every block and the castle falls.
  6. Stuck? Buy a clue or have a letter revealed with the coins you've won.
